Challenges

How to keep your IT services running smoothly in the long term

Many IT environments grow over the years. Teams expand systems, integrate platforms and automate processes – but without transparent structures, things can quickly become complex, unstable and costly.

Knowledge remains with individuals or is lost.

A growing number of systems makes it more difficult to implement IT security consistently.

CI/CD pipelines – Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery – are becoming almost impossible to trace.

Platforms are reaching their limits as the load increases.

Clear IT architecture is essential, particularly when setting up cloud infrastructures, platforms and CI/CD pipelines – especially given the increasing demands for compliance and security, as well as regulations such as NIS2.

We support you every step of the way. Together, we challenge the status quo and explore new approaches. In doing so, we rely on proven principles from IT service management and professional open-source technologies that can be deployed confidently and sustainably in the long term.

We empower you and hand over systems in such a way that you can continue on your own.

Our Approach

Our approach to reliable and transparent IT services.

The right solution for your needs. Based on open architectures.

Every IT landscape is different. That’s why we start by looking at your fundamentals: IT services, the systems you use and your platforms. On this basis, we develop an architecture using various open-source components that runs reliably and is easy to maintain. We continue to refine it until it reliably meets your requirements.

Agile in our collaboration. Clear in our structure. Reliable in our operations.

We remain flexible in our collaboration. We don’t believe in the phrase “that’s how we’ve always done it”. We challenge the status quo, explore new avenues and work with your teams to find solutions. As flexible as our collaboration is, the structures we build are just as robust. With an architecture that remains transparent and supports you in running your IT services in a stable and professional manner.

Understanding and designing. We create systems that remain transparent.

We want your teams to be able to operate and further develop platform architectures, DevOps structures and IT infrastructures with confidence. That is why we share our knowledge: through documentation, training and collaborative work on projects. Systems remain structured and maintainable through Infrastructure as Code – even long after our collaboration has ended.

Security and compliance. Built in at every stage.

Security and compliance are an integral part of the architecture – we do not add them as an afterthought. We integrate identity and access management, NIS-2, SBOM verification, vulnerability assessments and automated patch management into your systems, or provide them as separate services if required.

How we work

Initial consultation
